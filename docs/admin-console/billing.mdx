The Billing page lets you manage your platform subscription, monitor usage, and configure spending limits.

## Accessing Billing

Navigate to **Platform Admin → Setup → Billing** (or `/platform/setup/billing`).

<Note>
  Billing is available on Cloud and Enterprise editions. Community edition does not have billing management.
</Note>

## Subscription Information

If you have an active Stripe subscription, you'll see:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Plan** | Your current plan (Standard, Enterprise, AppSumo tier) |
| **Next Billing Date** | When your next payment is due |
| **Next Billing Amount** | The amount you'll be charged |
| **Cancel Date** | If scheduled for cancellation, when it ends |

### Billing Portal

Click **Access Billing Portal** to open the Stripe customer portal where you can:
- Update payment method
- View invoices and receipts
- Cancel or modify your subscription

## Active Flows

Your plan includes a base limit of active (enabled) flows. If you need more:

### Purchasing Additional Active Flows

1. Click **Add Active Flows** on the billing page
2. Choose the number of additional active flows
3. Complete the checkout process
4. Your limit is increased immediately

### Updating Active Flow Addon

If you already have extra active flows:
1. Click **Update** on the active flows section
2. Increase or decrease the addon quantity
3. Changes take effect immediately
4. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades apply at next billing cycle

## AI Credits

AI credits are consumed when universal AI pieces run in your flows. Each step that uses the AI piece costs 1 credit.

### Included AI Credits

Your plan includes a base amount of AI credits per billing period.

### AI Credit Overage

If you run out of included credits, you have three options:

| State | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| **Not Allowed** | AI steps fail when credits are exhausted (plan restriction) |
| **Allowed but Off** | Overage is available but you've chosen to disable it |
| **Allowed and On** | AI steps continue working, billed per-credit for overage |

### Configuring AI Overage

1. Toggle **Enable Usage-Based AI Credits** on the billing page
2. Set a **spending limit** (e.g., max 500 overage credits)
3. Usage beyond your plan's included credits is billed at the overage rate

<Warning>
  You cannot disable overage if you've already consumed overage credits in the current billing period. Wait for the next billing cycle or contact support.
</Warning>

### AI Credits Usage Table

For Enterprise plans, a detailed usage table shows:
- Per-project AI credit consumption
- Historical usage over time
- Credits remaining vs. limit

## License Keys (Enterprise)

Enterprise customers manage their license via the billing page:

1. Enter your license key
2. The system verifies the key and applies plan limits
3. License expiry date is shown

### Applying License Key by Email

Platform administrators can apply license keys programmatically via the admin API:

```bash
POST /v1/admin/platforms/apply-license-key
{
  "email": "admin@example.com",
  "licenseKey": "your-license-key"
}
```
